Privacy Policy — China Rail Buddy

中国高铁出行助手隐私政策

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Contact: ddxxdtt@gmail.com

English

China Rail Buddy is an independent guide. It is not a railway, a ticket office, or a payment service.

No account, no passport number, no bank card number, and no analytics. The app does not track you.

Photo translation reads text on this iPhone and does not upload the photo.

A train number, a seat, and the day's translation count stay on this iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple.

A correction email is only created if you tap Something's wrong, and it opens in Mail.

If an https address is configured, the app may download a newer copy of the guide. That request does not include your name, passport, or payment details.

Questions: ddxxdtt@gmail.com

简体中文

中国高铁出行助手是独立指南，不是铁路、售票处或支付服务。

没有账号，不收集护照号，不收集银行卡号，也没有统计。这个 App 不追踪你。

拍照翻译在这台 iPhone 上识别文字，不上传照片。

车次、座位和当天的翻译次数只留在这台 iPhone 上。购买由 Apple 处理。

只有点了「这里有误」才会生成更正邮件，并在「邮件」里打开。

如果配置了 https 地址，App 可能会下载更新的指南。这次请求不包含姓名、护照或支付信息。
